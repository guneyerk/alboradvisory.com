Why this program

In these markets, a signed order is not yet money

In a stable market, a signed order is almost as safe as cash. In a permacrisis market, its value keeps changing until the cash arrives, sometimes by more than the whole margin.

A permacrisis market is one where instability is the normal condition, year after year. Customers there raise pricing, credit and currency in the first meetings, and these decide whether a deal makes money. This program prepares teams for that conversation.

Three patterns, three ways a deal loses value

Steady decline

The currency loses value every month. Inflation and interest rates stay very high for years.

Turkey: inflation 29.77% in October 2026, policy rate 36%, lira near 50 per US dollar.Source: Trading Economics

Sudden step

The currency is held for a time, then falls sharply, and dollars and euros become scarce.

Nigeria: about 463 naira per US dollar before the June 2023 float, about 1,354 by May 2024.Source: BusinessDay

Weak payers

Inflation is moderate, but the currency swings and public customers pay late.

South Africa: 90,856 government invoices worth R15.5 billion unpaid past 30 days at the end of December 2025.Source: SAnews

The lines show the shape of each pattern, not data. Figures checked on 13 November 2026; market figures change monthly.

What your teams will learn

From the basics to a live deal

Part A teaches the finance basics and assumes no finance training. The workshops apply them to the deals sellers and marketers handle every week.

After the program, learners can

  • Quote in a moving currency, with a currency clause they can explain.
  • Check who will really pay, with what money, and set terms to match.
  • Build a payment schedule and choose the right security for the payer.
  • Find where margin is lost, defend a price and lead a price increase.
  • Offer a way to pay when a customer has the need but not the cash.
  • Recognize red lines on currency rules, sanctions and integrity.
  • Sales trackTake long deals to cash and review distributors.
  • Marketing trackReset prices and segment by ability to pay.

Who it is for

Sellers, key account managers, channel managers, marketers and pricing staff who work in or sell into markets such as Turkey, Egypt, Nigeria, Kenya and South Africa.

How it teaches

Every module has the same parts: the basics, a method, a worked example, practice and a task on a real customer. Five workshop modules add a role-play.
