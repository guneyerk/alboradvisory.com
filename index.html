The gap

Governance after the fact

Services

Four ways we help leadership teams restructure for AI

AI readiness review

A fixed-scope look at how ready your organization is to adopt AI at scale: where decisions sit, which roles change and what governance already exists.

What you leave with

  • A map of who decides what, today and with AI
  • The roles and processes that change first
  • A prioritized plan your leadership team agrees on

Operating model redesign

We redesign roles, decision rights, team structures and processes around the work AI changes, together with the people who will run them.

What you leave with

  • Decision rights for AI-informed work
  • Redesigned roles and team structures
  • A transition plan with named owners

AI governance

Policies, accountability and risk controls sized to your organization and aligned with emerging regulation such as the EU AI Act.

What you leave with

  • An AI policy people can actually follow
  • Clear accountability for each use case
  • Risk review built into how work is approved

Implementation and adoption

We stay through rollout, so pilots become the way the work is done and the change holds once we leave.

What you leave with

  • Pilots scaled with owners and success measures
  • Skills and change programs for the teams affected
  • Measures that show whether adoption is holding

About

We work where the change is still beginning

Albor is the first white light of dawn.

The word comes from the Latin albus, white. In Spanish, los albores also means the beginnings of something. That is the work: helping leadership teams see the AI-era organization while it is still taking shape, and redesign it before the change arrives.

  • With the people who will run itRoles and decisions are redesigned together with the teams who will live with them.
  • Governance from the startAccountability and risk controls are designed alongside the work instead of after the first incident.
  • Measured adoptionWe agree on measures up front and track whether the change is holding.
